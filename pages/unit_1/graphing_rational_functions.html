Graphing Rational Functions

Steps

  1. Factor
    both the numerator and the denominator
  2. Find Holes
    cancel any factors from step 1 => use those to find the holes by setting the factors to 0 and solving for x.
  3. Determine x-intercepts
    - set numerator to zero and solve for x for each factor
  4. Find the y-intercept
    by substituting x=0 into original function
  5. locate the vertical assymptotes
    by setting the denominator (of the simplified function) equal to zero
  6. find horizontal assymptotes
    • if the degree of the numerator is less than the degree of the denominator
      horizontal assymptote: y=0
    • if the degree of the numerator is EQUAL to the dgree of the denominator
      horizontal assymptote: y = (lead coefficient of numerator)/(lead coefficient of the denominator)
    • if the degree of the numerator is greater than the degree of the denominator
      NO horizontal assymptote but check for slant assymptote
  7. plot knowns from steps above
  8. use test points for around assymptotes and x-intercepts
